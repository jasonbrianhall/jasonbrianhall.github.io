🎵 Music and MIDI FM Synthesis Suite

Authentic OPL3 FM synthesis tools featuring the legendary Yamaha OPL3 sound chip that defined PC audio in the 1990s.

🎶 Zenamp

Audio Player

A feature-rich, cross-platform audio player combining modern format support with nostalgic MIDI synthesis, comprehensive karaoke capabilities, and 30+ mesmerizing visualizations.

  • 40+ Audio Visualizations (waveforms, kaleidoscopes, fractals, puzzle solvers)
  • Universal Format Support: WAV, FLAC, MP3, OGG, Opus, M4A, AAC, WMA, AIFF, MIDI, CD+G, LRC (supports other formats like APE from libavcodec)
  • Full Karaoke Support with CD+G graphics and LRC lyrics
  • 10-Band Equalizer with professional frequency control
  • Advanced Queue Management with sortable playlists
  • Includes some mini games playable against the AI including Chess, Checkers, Minesweeper, and Pong; other visualizations are interactive

🔄 MIDI to WAV Converter

Tool

High-quality offline conversion utility for converting MIDI files to WAV format using OPL3 FM synthesis.

  • 181 meticulously crafted FM instrument patches
  • General MIDI compatibility (128+ instruments)
  • Adjustable output volume (default 500%)
